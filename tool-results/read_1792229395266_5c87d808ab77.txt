  4862→**Noise formula — EXACT:**
  4863→- Height scale `s = 684.412` (the standard Minecraft 1.2+ terrain noise scale).
  4864→- Three primary noises:
  4865→  - `pnr` (perlinNoise1, 8 octaves) at scale `s/80 = 8.555` horizontal, `hs/160 = 4.277` vertical, `s/80` z — used for "interpolation" between the other two noises.
  4866→  - `ar` (lperlinNoise1, 16 octaves) at scale `s = 684.412` — low-frequency main noise.
  4867→  - `br` (lperlinNoise2, 16 octaves) at scale `s` — second low-frequency noise.
  4868→- `sr` (scaleNoise, 10 octaves) at scale 1.121 → biome-temperature-modulated "scale" factor.
  4869→- `dr` (depthNoise, 16 octaves) at scale 200 → biome depth modulation.
  4870→
  4871→For each column:
  4872→- `dd = (1 - (1 - (1 - downfall*temperature)^2)^2)` — wetter-biome amplification factor (Rainforest gets tall; Desert stays flat).
  4873→- `scale = clamp(scaleNoise/512 + 256) * dd, 0, 1) + 0.5` — overall terrain height scale.
  4874→- `depth = depthNoise * 3 - 2` then clamped/normalized — controls terrain base elevation.
  4875→- `yCenter = ySize/2 + depth*4` — center of terrain.
  4876→
  4877→For each y level (control point):
  4878→- `yOffs = (yy - yCenter) * 12 / scale`; if `yOffs < 0` → multiply by 4 (steeper below sea level).
  4879→- Blend `bb` (= `lperlinNoise1 / 512`) and `cc` (= `lperlinNoise2 / 512`) by `v = (perlinNoise1/10 + 1) / 2`, clamped to [0,1].
  4880→- `val = blend(bb, cc, v) - yOffs`.
  4881→- Top 4 control points (ySize-4 to ySize-1) blend linearly to -10 (forces sky at the top).
  4882→
  4883→Result `buffer[p] = val`: positive val = solid rock, negative = air or water.
